About Us

The Wheelaid App is designed to enhance mobility, accessibility, and independence for wheelchair users. Using GPS navigation and real-time data, the app provides accessible routes, locates wheelchair-friendly amenities, and promotes user engagement through feedback. It addresses challenges like limited accessibility information while advocating for inclusivity. We are constantly working to improve, positioning ourselves as a transformative tool for a more inclusive society.
